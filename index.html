01 Evening · 7:14 PM

Point.
Shoot.
Dinner logged.

Before bed, open Nocturne and photograph your plate. The camera focuses, you press the shutter, and the AI reads the meal — separating the foods and their likely ingredients on its own.

You never type an ingredient list. You never weigh anything. The photograph is the entry.

  • Foods identified automatically
  • Likely ingredients inferred — garlic, butter, cream
  • Timestamped to the minute it was eaten

Recognition is assistive. Anything the AI gets wrong takes one tap to correct.

02 Evening, later

If you consume it before bed, capture it.

Dinner is rarely the last thing. The square of chocolate at nine. The coffee you almost forgot about. The glass of wine while the credits roll. Those are often the entries that explain the night.

  • Dessert
  • Chocolate
  • Ice cream
  • Tea
  • Coffee
  • Soft drinks
  • Alcohol
  • Supplements
  • Late-night snacks

Every capture drops into tonight's timeline with its own time stamp — because when matters as much as what.

03 10:40 PM

Then Nocturne gets out of the way.

No wearable. No microphone in the room. No sleep stages guessed from a wrist. The evening is logged, and the app goes dark until morning.

Nothing to wear · nothing to charge

The alarm goes off and Nocturne asks two questions. A number out of ten, and whether you dreamed. It takes about eight seconds, before you are properly awake.

That single honest number, paired with last night's timeline, is the whole engine.

Dream tracking

Dreams are data,
not an afterthought.

Most tracking stops at duration. Nocturne records whether you dreamed, how vivid it was, how it felt, and — if you want — what you remember. Themes are extracted from your own words over time.

05 Over time

Every evening is a data point.

One night tells you nothing. Ten start to whisper. Nocturne lines up what you consumed, when you consumed it, how you slept and what you dreamed — then looks for the things that keep happening together.

What you consumed→When→How you slept→What you dreamed

Nocturne reports correlations observed in your own log. A pattern is not a cause: sleep is affected by stress, light, exercise, temperature, illness and much else. Nocturne does not diagnose sleep disorders, allergies, intolerances or any medical condition.

Sunday, 8:00 AM · Weekly report

Your week, decoded.

Seven evenings, seven mornings. Nocturne assembles them into one page you can read with a coffee.

No calorie counting.
No food diary.

Nocturne is not a nutrition app. There is no macro pie chart, no daily target, nothing to weigh and nothing to look up. You are not being scored on what you ate.

Just take a photo. The AI handles the initial recognition and logging — and everything after that is about how you slept.

Signal from noise

Your patterns become clearer with time.

Nocturne deliberately says very little in week one. Confidence is earned from repetition — the picture sharpens as the nights add up.

Your sleep is personal.

“Don't drink coffee at night” is not advice, it is a slogan. It says nothing about your 4 PM flat white, your decaf habit, or the fact that in your log the coffee nights score fine and the wine nights do not.

Population averages describe a crowd you are not in. Nocturne builds its insights from one history: yours.

Trust

Your meals.
Your sleep.
Your data.

This is a log of what you eat and how you rest — some of the most personal information there is. It is treated that way.

  • Yours by default

    Your photos, ratings and dream notes are yours. We do not sell them, and we do not share them with advertisers, insurers or employers.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest

    Images are processed for recognition and then stored against your account only.

  • Export or erase, any time

    One tap to download everything as a file you can read. One tap to delete it permanently, photos included.

  • Training is opt-in, never assumed

    Your images are not used to improve our models unless you explicitly turn that on. Off is the default.

  • Not a medical device

    Nocturne surfaces behavioural correlations from your own log. It does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders, allergies, intolerances or any medical condition — and it is not a substitute for a clinician.
